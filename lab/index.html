Lab

You found my demo page! Small interactive experiments live here, mostly physics toys built to see what can run well on a static github pages site. This page is somewhat unfinished but I will be linking to or embedding these demos into any relevant portfolio entries.

  • Cart-pole, where you balance an inverted pendulum by hand, or let an LQR do it and try to knock it over.
  • Stick the landing, a cartoon of Cooper launched on an arc starting out with a random tumble that you have to cancel and land optimally.
  • Suspension tuner, a car driving on a randomly generated road with a PID controller at each wheel that you can tune as it drives.
  • Double pendulum, a double pendulum that you can grab and throw, with an energy readout to watch the effects of damping.

    • Gimbal, a rocket hovering on a gimbaled engine, where the same actuator has to correct both drift and tipping at once, with an LQR controllerto hand it off to. (unpolished)

About

I'm a mechanical engineer with a BS and MS from UC Santa Barbara, focused on robotics, controls, and mechanical design. The problems I enjoy most are the ones where the mechanism and the controller have to be designed together, as the right hardware often makes the control problem much easier.

Education

Experience

Hawkes Lab, UC Santa Barbara · Nov 2022–Jun 2026

For my master's project I designed and built Cooper, a jumping robot that reorients itself in midair with a reaction wheel, owning the mechanical structure, BLDC actuation, IMU integration, and the embedded estimation and control running on an ESP32. Earlier in the lab I redesigned the energy transmission on a separate inertial jumping robot, improving its jump efficiency by 75% through rapid iteration on fabrication methods and contact features, and contributed structural and mechanical design to the lab's vine robot platforms, work that led to coauthorship on papers in IEEE RA-L and RAM. The research ran concurrently with coursework across both my undergraduate and graduate years.

CelLink · Summer 2025

As an equipment engineering intern I designed a tool-change mechanism for a robotic circuit assembler that cut cycle time by 70%, producing the CAD models, GD&T drawings, and tolerance stacks myself, and programmed and operated a Tormach CNC mill to fabricate tooling and fixturing in-house.

Analog Devices · Summer 2023

At their Beaverton wafer fab I led an overhaul of the facility's gas supply monitoring network under change control, reprogramming the PLC controllers in Ladder Logic and designing replacement displays, built with Rhino CAD and a CNC router, that report live pressure data over RS232 and alert technicians automatically when levels run low. The upgrades cut about 70 hours of manual supply rounds per week.

FIRST Robotics Competition · Sep 2017–Jun 2021

Four seasons on my high school's team, including two years as mechanical design team lead, where I built and taught a new member CAD and design curriculum covering powertrain layout, part and assembly design, and mechatronic power calculations, and led our prototyping efforts in the machine shop. It's where my interest in mechanical design started.

UC Santa Barbara · 2025–2026

Also tutored, graded, and taught as a TA at UCSB, across three separate quarter-long appointments: tutor for ENGR 3, reader for ME 163, and teaching assistant for ME 153.

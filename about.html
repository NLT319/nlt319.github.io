Experience

Hawkes Lab, UC Santa Barbara · Nov 2022–Jun 2026

For my master's project I designed and built Cooper, a jumping robot that reorients itself in midair with a reaction wheel, owning the mechanical structure, BLDC actuation, IMU integration, and the embedded estimation and control running on an ESP32. Earlier in the lab I inherited a separate inertial jumping robot from a labmate while he was focusing on other work, and had to do a full prototype cycle and redesign as it was underperforming its predicted jump height. I found two problems with root cause analysis: a transmission joint that was meant to pivot had been rigidly fixed by a constraint on the how the prototype fabrication, so the mechanism couldn't articulate effectively, and the flywheel mass wasn't tuned to the transmission's inertia. Redesigning both increased the robot's jump height 7x, from around 5 cm to 35 cm. I also contributed structural, mechanical, and experimental test design work to the lab's vine robot platforms, work that led to coauthorship on papers in IEEE RA-L and RAM. My research involvement ran concurrently with coursework every quarter across both my undergraduate and graduate years.

CelLink · Summer 2025

As an equipment engineering intern I designed a tool-change mechanism for a robotic circuit assembler that cut cycle time by 70%, producing the CAD models, GD&T drawings, and tolerance stacks myself, and programmed and operated a Tormach CNC mill to fabricate tooling and fixturing in-house.

Analog Devices · Summer 2023

At their Beaverton wafer fab I led an overhaul of the facility's gas supply monitoring network under change control, reprogramming the PLC controllers in Ladder Logic and designing replacement displays that report live pressure data over RS232 and alert technicians automatically when levels run low. The upgrades cut about 70 hours of manual supply rounds per week.

FIRST Robotics Competition · Sep 2017–Jun 2021

I spent four seasons on my high school's team, including two years as mechanical design team lead, where I built and taught a new member CAD and design curriculum covering powertrain layout, part and assembly design, and mechatronic power calculations. I also led our prototyping efforts, including block cad ideation, rapid iteration and fabricationin the machine shop, and mechanism design. This is where my interest in mechanical design began.
